STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

What is included in social media management?

Depending on your package, BrandSpring can provide strategy, content planning, branded creative, captions, scheduling, campaign support and performance reporting.

Which platforms can you manage?

Platform selection is based on your audience and business goals. Common options include Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok and Google Business Profile.

Do I approve content before it goes live?

Yes. Client review and approval are built into the BrandSpring process before scheduled content is published.

Can you create the content for my business?

Yes. Our services can include branded graphics, campaign concepts, captions and content planning so you are not left trying to build every post yourself.

Can social media help generate leads?

It can. Organic content builds visibility and credibility, while paid Facebook and Instagram campaigns can be used to target audiences and generate inquiries. Results depend on the offer, audience, budget, market and campaign.

Is advertising spend included?

No. Advertising budgets and other third-party costs are separate from the monthly management fee.

Where does BrandSpring provide social media management?

BrandSpring serves growing businesses across Ontario and can manage the process remotely through structured communication, content approval and reporting.
