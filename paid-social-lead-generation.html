FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

GOOD QUESTIONS.
STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

Which advertising platforms can you manage?

Platform choice depends on the audience and campaign. Facebook and Instagram are common starting points, with other channels considered when they fit the opportunity.

Is the ad budget included in my monthly fee?

No. Advertising spend is separate and is paid directly to the advertising platform.

Can you help improve the landing page or lead form?

Yes. Landing-page and conversion-path support can be included so traffic has a clear next step.

Can you track leads from the campaign?

Yes. We can set up practical lead tracking and reporting to make campaign results easier to understand.

Do you guarantee a specific number of leads?

Not by default. Lead volume varies based on budget, market, audience, offer and competition. Any specific performance commitment must be defined in writing before the campaign begins.
