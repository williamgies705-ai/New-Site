How to Rank Higher in Ontario Search Results

Local visibility comes from accurate information, relevant pages and proof that your business serves real customers.

By Bill Gies · 7 minute read

Complete your Google Business Profile

Use the correct primary category, service area, hours, phone number and website. Add real photos regularly and describe services in clear language. Keep this information consistent everywhere your business appears online.

Create pages around services, not keyword lists

Each important service deserves a useful page explaining who it is for, what is included, where it is available and what the customer should do next. Mention locations naturally when the page genuinely serves those areas.

Earn and answer reviews

Ask satisfied customers for honest reviews after the work is complete. Respond professionally to every review. Never manufacture feedback; genuine detail is more persuasive to customers and more credible to search engines.

Strengthen local signals

Accurate directory listings, links from local organizations, community partnerships and location-relevant content all help establish where the business operates. Make the site fast, mobile-friendly and easy to contact.

Start this weekCheck your business name, address or service area, phone number and hours across Google, Facebook and your website. Correct every mismatch.
