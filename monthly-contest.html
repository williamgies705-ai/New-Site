HOW IT WORKS

Each monthly contest will have its own prize and entry period. BrandSpring will publish the exact entry instructions before entries open.

  1. Check the current contest announcement.
  2. Follow the posted entry instructions during the contest period.
  3. Make sure you meet the eligibility requirements in the official rules.
  4. Eligible selected entrants must correctly answer a mathematical skill-testing question before being confirmed as a winner where required.
  5. The confirmed winner will be announced using the method described in that month's rules.

CONTEST RULES

No purchase necessary. Each BrandSpring Monthly Contest will have contest-specific official rules stating the prize, eligibility, opening and closing times, entry method, winner-selection process and any restrictions. Those rules will be posted before the contest opens and will govern that month's promotion.

Unless the contest-specific rules state otherwise, BrandSpring contests are intended for eligible Ontario residents of the age of majority. A mathematical skill-testing question may be required before a selected entrant can be declared a winner.
