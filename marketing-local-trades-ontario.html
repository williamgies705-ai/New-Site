LOCAL TRADES FAQ

STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

Can one page cover every city we serve?

The main page can explain your service area, but high-priority locations may benefit from genuinely useful location pages when the content and offering differ. We avoid mass-produced doorway pages.

What content should a contractor collect?

Project photos, short jobsite videos, before-and-after comparisons, common customer questions, service details and permissioned customer feedback are the most useful starting materials.

Can you market seasonal services?

Yes. Campaign calendars can align roofing, HVAC, landscaping, exterior work and other services with Ontario weather and buying cycles.
