Getting Started

What's the first step to working with Brand Spring?

Simple! Fill out our contact form or call us at 705-795-7147. We'll schedule a free 20-minute consultation where we learn about your business, goals, and current challenges. No pressure, no sales pitch—just a conversation.

How long does the onboarding process take?

About 1-2 weeks. We'll gather information about your brand, collect existing content, set up your accounts, and build your initial content calendar. You'll be active on social media and getting results before you know it.

Do I need to have existing social media accounts?

No! We can set up new accounts from scratch if needed. We'll handle everything—profile setup, optimization, branding, and strategy. Whether you're starting fresh or reviving old accounts, we've got you covered.

What information do you need from me to get started?

Mainly: your business mission, target audience, key services/products, brand colors/logo, existing photos/videos, and your main marketing goal. We'll ask for account access so we can post and manage on your behalf.

Services & Packages

What's the difference between Starter, Growth, and Pro packages?

Starter ($497/mo): 4 posts/week, single platform, basic community management.

Growth ($997/mo): 6 posts/week, multi-platform, paid ads, bi-weekly strategy calls.

Pro ($1,997/mo): Everything in Growth plus custom video content, advanced ads, weekly reviews, and premium features.

Can I customize a package to fit my needs?

Absolutely! If our standard packages don't quite fit, we offer custom solutions. Want more posts? Different platforms? Specific services? Let's build a plan that works for your budget and goals.

Do you offer one-time projects or only monthly retainers?

Both! We offer one-time projects like brand development, website design, or a one-month content sprint. However, for best results, we recommend ongoing management so your presence stays active and grows consistently.

Is advertising spend included in the price?

No, advertising spend is separate. Our fees cover strategy, content creation, posting, and management. Any paid ads on Instagram, Facebook, etc., are billed separately based on your budget and goals.

What platforms do you manage?

We specialize in Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and TikTok. We also manage YouTube, Twitter, and Pinterest depending on where your audience is. We'll help you focus on the platforms that actually drive results for your business.

Results & Timeline

How long does it take to see results?

You'll see engagement growth (likes, comments, shares) within 2-4 weeks of consistent posting. Follower growth takes 6-8 weeks. Lead generation and actual conversions typically follow as your audience grows and trust builds. Patience pays off!

What if I don't see results in the first month?

That's exactly why we have the Brand Spring Results Guarantee. Before we start, we agree on a clear, measurable result. If we don't hit it in month one, your monthly fee is paused until we do. We're confident in our work.

How do you measure success?

It depends on your goals. For some it's follower growth. For others it's engagement, website traffic, or actual sales. We'll define your KPIs upfront and track everything in monthly reports. You'll always know exactly what's working.

Will you help with paid advertising?

Yes! Growth and Pro packages include paid ad management. We'll create high-converting ads, target the right audience, test different versions, and optimize for best ROI. We handle everything—you just provide the budget.

Content & Posting

Do you create the content or just post mine?

We handle everything! We develop a content strategy, create original posts, design graphics, write captions, source photos/videos, and handle scheduling and posting. You don't need to provide content—we build it all from scratch based on your brand and audience.

Can I request specific content or topics?

Of course! We work with you to understand what matters most. You can suggest topics, request certain types of posts, or share content ideas. We blend your input with our strategy to create a content calendar that feels authentic to your brand.

How often will you post?

Starter: 4 posts/week. Growth: 6 posts/week. Pro: 6-8+ posts/week plus Stories/Reels. The schedule depends on your platform and audience, but consistency is key. We'll post at times when your audience is most active.

Do you create videos and Reels?

Growth and Pro packages include Reels and short-form video content. We create these from scratch or edit footage you provide. Videos perform better than static posts, so we prioritize them in your content strategy.

What if I don't like a post?

No problem! We'll revise it or create a different version. We want you to love everything we post under your brand. Your feedback helps us get closer to your vision and brand voice.

Account Access & Communication

Do you need access to my accounts?

Yes, we need admin or creator access to your social accounts so we can post, respond to comments, and manage your presence. We use industry-standard tools and never access other accounts or passwords. Your data is secure.

Can I keep full control of my accounts?

Absolutely. You maintain ownership and can remove our access anytime. We just need permissions to post and manage content. You can always review or approve posts before they go live if you prefer.

How often do we communicate?

Starter: Monthly performance reports. Growth: Bi-weekly strategy calls. Pro: Weekly reviews and check-ins. Plus you can reach us anytime with questions or feedback. We're responsive and always available.

Who will be managing my account?

Your account is managed by Bill Gies and the Brand Spring team. You'll have a dedicated contact who knows your business, goals, and preferences. Consistency matters, and you'll work with the same team throughout.

Billing & Contracts

What are your payment terms?

We bill monthly, due on the 1st of each month. Payments are typically made via bank transfer or credit card. Invoices are sent 3-5 days before the billing date so you know what to expect.

Are there long-term contracts?

No! Most clients are on month-to-month agreements with no lock-in periods. We believe in earning your business every month. If you want to pause or cancel, just give 30 days notice.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. With 30 days written notice, you can pause or cancel your service. We'll transition everything back to you—all content calendars, assets, accounts—so there's zero disruption.

What if I want to scale up or down mid-month?

Easy! You can upgrade or downgrade anytime. We'll prorate the charges so you only pay for what you use. Just let us know and we'll adjust immediately.

Do you offer discounts for longer commitments?

We sometimes offer discounts for 3, 6, or 12-month prepayments. If you're interested in a longer commitment and want a better rate, let's chat! Contact us to discuss options.
